# NTT DATA IDP

Welcome to the **NTT DATA IDP** GitHub organization!

## About Us

NTT DATA IDP (Internal Developer Platform) is the organization dedicated to building and maintaining the internal developer platform at NTT DATA. Our mission is to empower development teams with the tools, infrastructure, and best practices they need to build, deploy, and operate software efficiently and reliably.

## What We Do

- **Developer Experience**: We design and deliver self-service platforms that reduce friction for engineering teams.
- **Infrastructure Automation**: We provide infrastructure-as-code templates, CI/CD pipelines, and automation tooling.
- **Standards & Governance**: We establish and maintain engineering standards, security policies, and compliance guardrails across the organization.
- **Shared Services**: We offer shared components, libraries, and services to help teams avoid duplicated effort and focus on delivering value.

## Getting Started

If you are a member of the NTT DATA organization and want to contribute or collaborate:

1. Browse our repositories to find the relevant project or tooling.
2. Check each repository's `README.md` and contributing guidelines for setup instructions.
3. Reach out to the IDP team through internal channels if you have questions or need support.

## Contact

For questions, suggestions, or support, please reach out through the official internal communication channels of NTT DATA.
